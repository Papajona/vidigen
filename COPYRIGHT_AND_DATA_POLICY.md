# Copyright, licensing and training-data policy

Vidigen should not automatically scrape or train on arbitrary online videos, images, model weights, code, or datasets. Publicly reachable does not mean public-domain or licensed for training.

The resource-learning system should ingest only resources that the user has the right to use, or that have a license permitting the intended use. Store the source URL, author, license, retrieval date, and checksum where practical.

For open-source/open-weight models, verify the exact repository/checkpoint license and any commercial-use restrictions before distribution. Model weights, datasets, sample media, and code can have different licenses.

Do not remove watermarks or copyright-management information. Do not claim ownership of generated output when the upstream model license or source material says otherwise.
